\section*{\texorpdfstring{Lehmer's Mahler Measure Problem}{Lehmer's Mahler Measure Problem}}\label{206}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
If a monic polynomial $P\in{\mathbb{Z}}[X]$ factors as
$P(X)=\prod_{j=1}^d(X-\alpha_j)$ over ${\mathbb{C}}$, its multiplicative Mahler measure is
\[
 M(P)=\prod_{j=1}^d\max\{1,|\alpha_j|\}.
\]
The selected gap assertion is that an absolute ${\varepsilon}_0>0$ exists with
$M(P)>1\Longrightarrow M(P)\ge1+{\varepsilon}_0$ for every monic integer polynomial of arbitrary degree. Equivalently, either establish a gap immediately above one or produce a sequence with $M(P_j)>1$ and $M(P_j)\to1$. No fixed-degree bound can substitute for an absolute bound across all degrees, and the polynomials need not be irreducible.
\par\smallskip\textit{Formulation and concept references:} \href{https://mathworld.wolfram.com/LehmersMahlerMeasureProblem.html}{[S1]}.

\subsection{Short English statement}
Is there a universal gap above one among Mahler measures of monic integer polynomials, or can such measures approach one from above?
