\section*{\texorpdfstring{Exact Unassisted Quantum Capacity of the Qubit Depolarizing Channel}{Exact Unassisted Quantum Capacity of the Qubit Depolarizing Channel}}\label{273}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a qubit state $\rho$, the depolarizing channel is
$\mathcal D_p(\rho)=(1-p)\rho+pI_2/2$, $0\le p\le1$. A block code uses a completely positive trace-preserving encoder into $n$ qubits and a decoder after $\mathcal D_p^{\otimes n}$. A rate $R$ is achievable if $k_n/n\to R$ and the entanglement-transmission error for $k_n$ logical qubits tends to zero. The unassisted quantum capacity is the supremum of achievable rates. Determine
\[
 Q(\mathcal D_p)\quad\text{for every }p\in[0,1].
\]
No preshared entanglement or auxiliary classical messages are available. The parameter $p$ is the replacement-by-maximally-mixed-state probability in this formula, not another convention's total Pauli-error probability.
\par\smallskip\textit{Formulation and concept references:} \href{https://www.preskill.caltech.edu/ph219/chap10_6A_2025.pdf}{[S1]}.

\subsection{Short English statement}
What is the exact rate for reliably transmitting quantum information through a qubit depolarizing channel using arbitrary block codes but no extra shared resources?
