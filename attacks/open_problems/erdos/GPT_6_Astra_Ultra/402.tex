\section*{Erd\H{o}s Problem 402}

\paragraph{Statement and status.}
For every nonempty finite set \(A\) of positive integers, must there be
\(a,b\in A\) with \(a/\gcd(a,b)\geq |A|\)?
The answer is known to be yes, by Balasubramanian and Soundararajan.
The general sharp inequality is unresolved in this attempt; the argument
below gives a weaker universal bound, exact special cases, and a useful
duality.

\paragraph{A universal elementary bound.}
Put \(n=|A|\), \(m=\min A\), and
\[
 M=\max_{a,b\in A}\frac{a}{\gcd(a,b)}.
\]
Reduce each fraction \(a/m\) to \(p/q\). Then
\[
 1\leq q\leq p\leq M,\qquad \gcd(p,q)=1.
\]
The upper bound on \(p\) follows from the pair \((a,m)\), and distinct
members of \(A\) give distinct fractions. With \(\varphi(1)=1\), this gives
\[
 n\leq\sum_{p=1}^{M}\varphi(p)
 \leq1+\frac{M(M-1)}2.
\]
Therefore
\[
 M\geq
 \left\lceil\frac{1+\sqrt{8n-7}}2\right\rceil .
 \tag{1}
\]
This is a genuine pairwise gcd conclusion, but its order \(\sqrt n\)
does not reach the required \(n\).

\paragraph{Sharp special cases.}
If \(\min A\) divides every element of \(A\), divide by it. The normalized
set contains \(1\) and has \(n\) distinct positive elements, so its maximum
is at least \(n\). The pair consisting of this maximum and \(1\) proves
\(M\geq n\). In particular, this covers every divisibility chain.
For a pairwise coprime set with \(n\geq2\), pair its largest element
with any other member to obtain the same conclusion. The case \(n=1\)
has \(M=1\).

\paragraph{Reciprocal duality.}
Let \(L=\operatorname{lcm}(A)\) and \(A^*=\{L/a:a\in A\}\).
For \(a,b\in A\),
\[
 \gcd(L/a,L/b)=\frac{L}{\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)},\qquad
 \frac{L/a}{\gcd(L/a,L/b)}=\frac{b}{\gcd(a,b)}.
\]
Thus \(|A^*|=|A|\) and \(M(A^*)=M(A)\). Every proved special case
therefore transfers to its reciprocal dual. The sets
\(\{1,\ldots,n\}\) have \(M=n\), since no numerator can exceed \(n\)
and the pair \((n,1)\) attains it. Their reciprocal duals are also sharp.

\paragraph{Remaining gap and sources.}
The missing step is to replace the quadratic counting estimate for reduced
fractions by the sharp bound \(|A|\leq M\) for arbitrary \(A\).
The known solution is R.~Balasubramanian and K.~Soundararajan,
\emph{On a conjecture of R.~L.~Graham}, Acta Arithmetica 75 (1996),
1--38, \url{https://eudml.org/doc/206861}.
Current statement: \url{https://www.erdosproblems.com/402},
checked 24 September 2026. Its full theorem is not used as a substitute
for the missing proof here.

\paragraph{Completion estimate.}
The universal weaker bound and sharp families are proved; the general sharp step is missing.
