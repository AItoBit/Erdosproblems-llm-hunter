\subsection*{2) WORK}
Fix a vertex $v$. At most three other vertices can be at any one distance from $v$: four would lie on the circle with that center and radius. If $d$ distances occur in the whole configuration, it follows that
\[
n-1\le3d,\qquad h(n)\ge\left\lceil\frac{n-1}{3}\right\rceil.
\]
The argument uses only the no-four-concyclic condition. Equivalently, each fixed-distance graph has maximum degree at most three, so its at most $3n/2$ edges cannot cover more than that many of the $\binom n2$ pairs. This improves the elementary scale quoted in the supplied discussion but does not reach the conjectured superlinear scale.

I also rechecked its five coordinates
\[
(0,0),(1,0),(\sqrt3/2,1/2),(1/2,1-\sqrt3/2),(0,1)
\]
using rational arithmetic in $\mathbb Q(\sqrt3)$. The ten squared distances have multiplicities $5,4,1$ at $1,2-\sqrt3,2$. All ten signed-area determinants and all five circle determinants are nonzero. Thus the concrete upper bound $h(5)\le3$ is certified. The independently executed code and exact determinant values are in \texttt{verification/batch\_1\_checks.py} and its JSON output. This verifies an existing example rather than claiming a new one.
