\section{WORK: KEEPING THE ENDPOINT LOSSES}
For $1\le a_1<\cdots<a_m\le N$ with distinct positive differences and $1\le u<m$, put $T=um-u(u+1)/2$. The supplied telescoping argument can retain the spacing of the endpoints:
\[
\sum_{i=1}^{m-k}(a_{i+k}-a_i)
=\sum_{j=m-k+1}^m a_j-\sum_{j=1}^k a_j
\le kN-k^2.
\]
Indeed the first sum is at most $kN-k(k-1)/2$ and the second at least $k(k+1)/2$. Distinctness gives a lower bound $T(T+1)/2$ on the combined difference sum. Consequently
\[
T(T+1)\le u(u+1)\left(N-\frac{2u+1}{3}\right).
\]
Equivalently, every such $m$ satisfies the explicit finite bound
\[
m\le\frac{u+1}{2}+
\frac{\sqrt{1+4u(u+1)(N-(2u+1)/3)}-1}{2u}
\quad(1\le u<m).
\]
This gives a direct integer certificate for ruling out proposed small Sidon configurations, retaining information discarded by the coarser $kN$ estimate.
