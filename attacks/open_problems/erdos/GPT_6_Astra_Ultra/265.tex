\section{WORK: TWO DENOMINATOR CONSTRAINTS}
Suppose $a_{n+1}\ge2a_n$ eventually and the two sums are $p/q$ and $r/s$, respectively, with positive integer denominators $q,s$. Put
\[
Q_n=\prod_{i\le n}a_i,\qquad R_n=\prod_{i\le n}(a_i-1).
\]
For all sufficiently large $n$, positivity and rationality of the tails give
\[
\frac1{qQ_n}\le\sum_{i>n}\frac1{a_i}\le\frac2{a_{n+1}},
\qquad a_{n+1}\le2qQ_n.
\]
Also $a_i-1\ge a_i/2$, so
\[
\frac1{sR_n}\le\sum_{i>n}\frac1{a_i-1}\le\frac4{a_{n+1}},
\qquad a_{n+1}\le4sR_n.
\]
Thus both constraints must hold simultaneously. In particular no such sequence can satisfy $a_{n+1}\ge a_n^{2+\delta}$ eventually for fixed $\delta>0$. Indeed, writing $b_n=\log a_n$ and summing the backward geometric bound gives
\[
\log Q_n\le O(1)+\frac{2+\delta}{1+\delta}b_n.
\]
But $\log a_{n+1}\ge(2+\delta)b_n$, contradicting $a_{n+1}\le2qQ_n$ as $n\to\infty$.
