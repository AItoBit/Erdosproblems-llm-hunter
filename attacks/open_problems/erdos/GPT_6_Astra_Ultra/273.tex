Suppose a cover has at most nineteen moduli. It must include each of $4,6,10$. Indeed, if it omits any one of them, its weight is at most the sum of the nineteen largest reciprocal weights available with that omission. The largest of these three possibilities omits $10$, giving
\[
w(M)\le S_{20}-\frac1{10}
=\frac{90391711}{96144048}<1,
\]
a contradiction.

Consider now the three selected congruences modulo $4,6,10$. Two of their residues have the same parity. Every pair of these three moduli has greatest common divisor two, so that pair of congruences intersects, with intersection density
\[
\frac1{\operatorname{lcm}(m,n)}=\frac2{mn}\ge\frac1{30}.
\]
First combine this pair exactly, then apply the union bound to all remaining classes. The total covered density is at most
\[
w(M)-\frac1{30}\le S_{19}-\frac1{30}<1,
\]
contradicting coverage. Thus every such covering system has at least twenty classes. Distinctness then forces at least one modulus to be $78$ or larger.
