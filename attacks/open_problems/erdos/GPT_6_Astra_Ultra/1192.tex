\section*{Erd\H{o}s Problem 1192 --- GPT 6 Astra Ultra partial attempt}


\subsection*{FORMAL RESTATEMENT}
For each integer $r\geq2$, can one choose $A\subseteq\mathbb N$ and a constant $C$ with the following properties? If
\[f_r(n)=\#\{(a_1,\ldots,a_r)\in A^r:a_1+\cdots+a_r=n\},\]
then $f_r(n)>0$ for every sufficiently large $n$, and
\[\sum_{n\leq x}f_r(n)^2\leq Cx\]
for every $x\geq1$.

\subsection*{CONTEXT AND SCOPE}
This replaces the earlier statement-only record with an elementary mathematical attempt. The arguments below establish only their stated partial results; no novelty or full solution is claimed.

Representations are written as ordered tuples. The existence question with fixed r is unchanged by using unordered representations, up to a factor at most r! in the count. The official page records a positive answer for r=2.

\subsection*{ATTACK PLAN AND WORK}
Suppose such a set exists and write $S_r(x)=\sum_{n\le x}f_r(n)$.
Eventual coverage and the proposed square-mean bound imply
\[x-O(1)\le S_r(x)\le\sqrt{\lfloor x\rfloor\sum_{n\le x}f_r(n)^2}
\le\sqrt C\,x.\tag{1}\]
There are at most $A(x)^r$ contributing ordered tuples, so (1) gives
$A(x)\ge(x-O(1))^{1/r}$. Conversely every tuple from $A\cap[1,y]$ has sum at
most $ry$, hence
\[A(y)^r\le S_r(ry)\le r\sqrt C\,y.\tag{2}\]
Any solution must therefore live in the critical density regime
\[A(y)\asymp y^{1/r}.\tag{3}\]
The proof treats ordered representations consistently; no factor of $r!$ is
lost in either tuple count.

The same energy bound controls large representation counts:
\[|\{n\le x:f_r(n)\ge T\}|\le Cx/T^2.\tag{4}\]
It does not prohibit rare large counts, and replacing it by a uniform bound
on every $f_r(n)$ would change the problem. At the other extreme, proving
(3) alone does not prevent infinitely many uncovered integers.

\subsection*{VERIFICATION AND REMAINING GAP}
Construct a basis in the critical regime while controlling its representation
energy. The necessary growth window and tail estimate do not supply coverage
or the square-mean bound for an actual new set.
