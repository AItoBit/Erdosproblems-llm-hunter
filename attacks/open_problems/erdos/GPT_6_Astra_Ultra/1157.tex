\section*{Erd\H{o}s Problem 1157 --- GPT 6 Astra Ultra partial attempt}


\subsection*{FORMAL RESTATEMENT}
Fix parameters $r,k,s\geq2$, and let $\mathcal F_{r,k,s}$ consist of all $r$-uniform hypergraphs on $k$ vertices with $s$ edges. Determine
\[
\operatorname{ex}_r(n,\mathcal F_{r,k,s}),
\]
the largest edge count of an $n$-vertex $r$-uniform hypergraph containing no member of that family as a subhypergraph.

\subsection*{CONTEXT AND SCOPE}
This replaces the earlier statement-only record with an elementary mathematical attempt. The arguments below establish only their stated partial results; no novelty or full solution is claimed.

The indexed official opening says t,k,r>=2 but subsequently uses s for the number of edges. This paraphrase consistently names that edge parameter s; it does not repeat the separate conjectural formula in the remarks, where another uniformity-index typo appears.

\subsection*{ATTACK PLAN AND WORK}
Assume $n\ge k\ge r$ and $2\le s\le\binom kr$; outside the feasible range
the forbidden family may be empty. Being free of this family is equivalent to
every $k$-vertex set containing at most $s-1$ edges. Double counting pairs
$(e,U)$ with $e\subseteq U$ and $|U|=k$ gives the exact upper bound
\[e(H)\binom{n-r}{k-r}\le(s-1)\binom nk,
\qquad e(H)\le\frac{s-1}{\binom kr}\binom nr.\tag{1}\]

An alteration argument gives a complementary, usually much smaller, lower bound.
Choose each $r$-set independently with probability $p$. There are $\binom nr p$
expected edges. The expected number of forbidden configurations is at most
$\binom nk\binom{\binom kr}{s}p^s$. Delete one edge from each surviving forbidden
configuration; this destroys all of them, since deletion cannot create a new one.
For $a=(k-r)/(s-1)$ and $p=c n^{-a}$ with a sufficiently small fixed $c>0$,
both expectation terms have power $n^{r-a}$, with coefficients proportional
to $c$ and $c^s$. Their difference is positive. Hence, when $r-a>0$,
\[\operatorname{ex}_r(n,\mathcal F_{r,k,s})\gg n^{r-(k-r)/(s-1)}.\tag{2}\]
The constants depend on the fixed parameters. A nonpositive exponent is not
being interpreted as a growing lower bound.

\subsection*{VERIFICATION AND REMAINING GAP}
Close the exponent and constant gaps between (1) and (2) in the nontrivial
parameter regimes. These are elementary general bounds, not a determination
of the requested extremal function.
