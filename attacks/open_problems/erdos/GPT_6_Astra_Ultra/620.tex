\subsection*{2) WORK: MATCHING THE LITERATURE TO THE TARGET}
Morris, Sahasrabudhe and Verstra\"ete, \emph{On the Erd\H{o}s--Rogers function}, arXiv:2607.16118v1, submitted 2026-07-17, state in Theorem 1.1 that for every fixed $s\ge2$,
\[
f_{s,s+1}(n)=\Theta(\sqrt{n\log n}).
\]
Their Theorem 3.1 quantifies the upper bound for every sufficiently large $n$, not only a subsequence: there is an absolute $C$ such that for $n\ge s^C$ an $n$-vertex $K_{s+1}$-free graph exists whose every vertex set of size at least $Cs^3\sqrt{n\log n}$ contains a $K_s$. Substituting $s=3$ gives $f_{3,4}(n)=O(\sqrt{n\log n})$.

The lower-bound reduction can be checked directly. Joret, Micek, Reed and Smid's published clique-colouring theorem, Corollary 2, gives a vertex colouring of any $n$-vertex graph with at most $C_0\sqrt{n/\log n}$ colours such that no inclusion-wise maximal clique of size at least two is monochromatic. In a $K_4$-free graph every triangle is such a maximal clique: an additional vertex extending it would create a $K_4$. Therefore each colour class induces a triangle-free graph. By the pigeonhole principle the largest class has at least
\[
\frac{n}{C_0\sqrt{n/\log n}}=C_0^{-1}\sqrt{n\log n}
\]
vertices. Combining the two named results gives
\[
\boxed{f_{3,4}(n)=\Theta(\sqrt{n\log n})}.
\]
