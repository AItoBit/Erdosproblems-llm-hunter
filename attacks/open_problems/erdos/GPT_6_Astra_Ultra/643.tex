Indeed, given $A\sqcup B=C\sqcup D$, take the four intersections $A\cap C,A\cap D,B\cap C,B\cap D$. If $r=|A\cap C|$, their sizes are $r,t-r,t-r,r$. Distinctness of the edges excludes $r=0,t$. Interchanging $C,D$ replaces $r$ by $t-r$, allowing $r\le t/2$. Conversely the rows and columns of each such block construction are distinct disjoint complementary edge pairs with the same union. Therefore
\[
f(n;t)-1=\operatorname{ex}\bigl(n,\{H_{t,r}:1\le r\le\lfloor t/2\rfloor\}\bigr).
