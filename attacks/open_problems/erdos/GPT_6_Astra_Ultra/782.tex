\section{WORK: CORRECTING AND CERTIFYING THE PELL FAMILY}
In fact
\[
1068^2-73\cdot125^2=-1,
\]
not $+1$. Nevertheless this yields a completely explicit infinite width-one family. Define $x_0=1,y_0=0$ and
\[
x_{r+1}=1068x_r+9125y_r,\qquad
y_{r+1}=125x_r+1068y_r.
\]
Multiplication of $x_r+y_r\sqrt{73}$ by $1068+125\sqrt{73}$ proves inductively
\[
x_r^2-73y_r^2=(-1)^r.
\]
For $r\ge1$, the four positive squares
\[
y_r^2,\quad25y_r^2,\quad49y_r^2,\quad x_r^2
\]
have consecutive gaps $24y_r^2,24y_r^2,24y_r^2+(-1)^r$. All are positive, and their spread is exactly one. The recurrence strictly increases $y_r$, so the families are distinct. Squaring the initial unit gives the genuine positive-norm solution $(x_2,y_2)=(2281249,267000)$. Thus the sign error does not invalidate the intended infinite construction; it changes which of the two adjacent possible gaps occurs.
As a separate elementary check, the five squares $1,9,16,25,36$ have gaps $8,7,9,11$, hence spread four. This improves the width-six example in the supplied text, but is only a finite witness and is not asserted to be new.
