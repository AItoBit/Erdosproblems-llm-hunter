\section*{Erd\H{o}s Problem 1183 --- GPT 6 Astra Ultra partial attempt}


\subsection*{FORMAL RESTATEMENT}
For every red--blue colouring of $\mathcal P([n])$, consider the largest monochromatic subfamily closed under both binary union and binary intersection. Let $f(n)$ be the minimum of this largest size over all such colourings. Define $F(n)$ in the same way with only closure under union required. Estimate both functions. In particular, are the inequalities
\[F(n)\geq n^{\omega(n)}\quad\text{for some }\omega(n)\to\infty,
\qquad F(n)=\exp(o(n))\]
valid?

\subsection*{ATTACK PLAN AND WORK}
Take the chain
$\varnothing\subset\{1\}\subset\{1,2\}\subset\cdots\subset[n]$.
At least $\lceil(n+1)/2\rceil$ of its members have the same color.
Any subfamily of a chain is closed under binary union and intersection:
these operations simply select the larger and smaller of their two inputs.
Consequently
\[\lceil(n+1)/2\rceil\le f(n)\le F(n).\tag{1}\]
No closure under the empty union is being inserted into the problem.

There is a structural certificate useful for searches beyond this bound.
For a nonempty finite union-closed family $\mathcal A$, the union $U$ of all
its members belongs to $\mathcal A$, by repeatedly applying binary closure.
If it is also intersection-closed, the intersection $I$ belongs as well, and
every member lies in the interval $[I,U]$ of the Boolean lattice. Hence
\[|\mathcal A|\le2^{|U\setminus I|}.\tag{2}\]
The converse would be false: having same-colored endpoints does not force
the intervening interval to be monochromatic or closed in a chosen subfamily.

Thus a large two-operation family needs a large interval between its extremal
sets, but proving such an interval exists cannot be replaced by a count of
same-colored endpoints. For union closure alone, even the common intersection
need not be a family member.

\subsection*{VERIFICATION AND REMAINING GAP}
Force monochromatic union-closed families of superpolynomial size and find
matching subexponential upper constructions. The chain argument and interval
certificate are linear-scale baseline tools only.
