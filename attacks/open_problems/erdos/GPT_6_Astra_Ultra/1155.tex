\section*{Erd\H{o}s Problem 1155 --- GPT 6 Astra Ultra partial attempt}


\subsection*{FORMAL RESTATEMENT}
Starting from $K_n$, repeatedly select uniformly among the triangles currently present and remove the selected triangle's three edges. Stop when no triangle remains, and denote the final number of edges by $f(n)$. Determine the typical structure and parameters of the terminal graph. In particular, are
\[
\mathbb E f(n)\asymp n^{3/2}\qquad\text{and}\qquad f(n)\ll n^{3/2}
\]
valid, with the latter holding asymptotically almost surely?

\subsection*{CONTEXT AND SCOPE}
This replaces the earlier statement-only record with an elementary mathematical attempt. The arguments below establish only their stated partial results; no novelty or full solution is claimed.

The source uses 'almost surely' in the random-graph asymptotic sense, as its discussion of probabilities tending to one makes explicit; no coupling of all graph sizes is specified.

\subsection*{ATTACK PLAN AND WORK}
Every triangle removal lowers the degree of each of its three vertices by two.
Thus every terminal graph $H$ satisfies
\[d_H(v)\equiv n-1\pmod2,\qquad e(H)\equiv\binom n2\pmod3.\tag{1}\]
In particular, when $n$ is even, every terminal degree is odd and positive, so
$e(H)\ge n/2$ deterministically. These constraints hold for every removal order.

One also has the elementary upper bound $e(H)\le\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor$.
For completeness, if $uv$ is an edge in a triangle-free graph then its two
neighborhoods are disjoint, so $d(u)+d(v)\le n$. Summing over edges gives
$\sum_vd(v)^2\le ne(H)$. Cauchy--Schwarz gives
$4e(H)^2/n\le\sum_vd(v)^2$, proving the bound when $e(H)>0$; the empty case is immediate.

These invariants provide useful checks for any simulation and show why termination
cannot be equated with an empty graph. But the interval from a linear lower bound
to a quadratic upper bound includes the proposed $n^{3/2}$ scale. Establishing
that scale needs information about the random process before termination, not
just constraints on its possible terminal states.

\subsection*{VERIFICATION AND REMAINING GAP}
Control the evolving triangle counts and their fluctuations far enough into
the process to determine the residual edge scale and expectation. The deterministic
invariants above do not supply either typical behavior or uniform integrability.
