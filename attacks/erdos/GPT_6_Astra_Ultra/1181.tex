\subsection*{FORMAL RESTATEMENT}
For $n\in\mathbb N$ and $k\geq1$, define $q(n,k)$ as the smallest prime not dividing $\prod_{1\leq i\leq k}(n+i)$, with $i$ integral. Is there an absolute constant $c>0$ such that
\[q(n,\log n)<(1-c)(\log n)^2\]
for every sufficiently large integer $n$?
