\subsection*{FORMAL RESTATEMENT}
For each integer $r\geq2$, can one choose $A\subseteq\mathbb N$ and a constant $C$ with the following properties? If
\[f_r(n)=\#\{(a_1,\ldots,a_r)\in A^r:a_1+\cdots+a_r=n\},\]
then $f_r(n)>0$ for every sufficiently large $n$, and
\[\sum_{n\leq x}f_r(n)^2\leq Cx\]
for every $x\geq1$.
