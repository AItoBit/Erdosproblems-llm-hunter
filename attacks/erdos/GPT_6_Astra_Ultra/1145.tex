\subsection*{FORMAL RESTATEMENT}
Suppose $A=\{a_1<a_2<\cdots\}$ and $B=\{b_1<b_2<\cdots\}$ are infinite sets of positive integers, $a_n/b_n\to1$, and every sufficiently large integer belongs to $A+B$. With
\[
r_{A,B}(m)=|\{(a,b)\in A\times B:a+b=m\}|,
\]
must $\limsup_{m\to\infty}r_{A,B}(m)=+\infty$?
