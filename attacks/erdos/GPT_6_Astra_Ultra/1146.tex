\section*{Erd\H{o}s Problem 1146 --- GPT 6 Astra Ultra partial attempt}


\subsection*{FORMAL RESTATEMENT}
For $E\subseteq\mathbb N$, put
\[
d_s(E)=\inf_{N\geq1}\frac{|E\cap\{1,\ldots,N\}|}{N}.
\]
Let $A=\{2^m3^n:m,n\geq0\}$. Is $A$ an essential component: for every $B\subseteq\mathbb N$ with $0<d_s(B)<1$, does
\[
d_s\bigl((A\cup\{0\})+(B\cup\{0\})\bigr)>d_s(B)
\]
hold?

\subsection*{CONTEXT AND SCOPE}
This replaces the earlier statement-only record with an elementary mathematical attempt. The arguments below establish only their stated partial results; no novelty or full solution is claimed.

The adjoined zero terms are the sumset convention explicitly used by the formalization for Schnirelmann density.

\subsection*{ATTACK PLAN AND WORK}
Since $1\in A$, the target sumset contains $B\cup(B+1)$, as well as zero.
This proves the essential-component inequality for a concrete infinite family
of test sets. For $q\ge2$ let $B_q$ be the positive integers not divisible by $q$.
Then
\[d_s(B_q)=\inf_{N\ge1}\left(1-\frac{\lfloor N/q\rfloor}{N}\right)=1-1/q.\]
Every positive integer outside $B_q$ is a positive multiple of $q$; its predecessor
lies in $B_q$. Therefore $B_q\cup(B_q+1)=\mathbb N_{\ge1}$ and
\[d_s((A\cup\{0\})+(B_q\cup\{0\}))=1>d_s(B_q).\tag{1}\]
More generally (1) holds for any $B$ of density strictly below one whose complement
contains no two consecutive positive integers, provided $1\in B$.
Positive Schnirelmann density itself forces $1\in B$ by considering $N=1$.

This also explains a limitation of the elementary shift argument. For a general
$B$, long consecutive missing blocks can remain after adding the single element
1. The full question requires the extra shifts $2^m3^n$, with a strict improvement
uniform over all initial intervals in the infimum, not just an improvement in
asymptotic density along selected intervals.

\subsection*{VERIFICATION AND REMAINING GAP}
Establish a strict increase for every positive-Schnirelmann-density set.
The proved family has particularly simple missing residues; no reduction of
arbitrary $B$ to that family is supplied.
