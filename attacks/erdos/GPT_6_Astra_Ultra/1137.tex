\section*{Erd\H{o}s Problem 1137 --- GPT 6 Astra Ultra partial attempt}


\subsection*{FORMAL RESTATEMENT}
Write $p_n$ for the $n$th prime and $d_n=p_{n+1}-p_n$. Determine whether
\[
\frac{\max_{2\leq n<x}d_nd_{n-1}}{\bigl(\max_{1\leq n<x}d_n\bigr)^2}\longrightarrow0
\qquad(x\to\infty).
\]

\subsection*{CONTEXT AND SCOPE}
This replaces the earlier statement-only record with an elementary mathematical attempt. The arguments below establish only their stated partial results; no novelty or full solution is claimed.

The displayed lower index bounds make the adjacent gaps defined under one-based prime indexing; finite initial-index conventions do not affect the limiting question.

\subsection*{ATTACK PLAN AND WORK}
Put $D_N=\max_{1\le j\le N}d_j$ and introduce
\[
A_N=D_N^{-2}\max_{2\le j\le N}d_{j-1}d_j,
\qquad B_N=D_N^{-1}\max_{2\le j\le N}\min(d_{j-1},d_j).
\]
Then the following exact comparison holds:
\[ B_N^2\le A_N\le B_N. \tag{1}\]
Indeed, for positive $u,v\le D_N$, $\min(u,v)^2\le uv\le D_N\min(u,v)$;
taking maxima gives (1). Consequently the original limit is zero if and only
if $B_N\to0$. Equivalently, for every $\epsilon>0$, all sufficiently long prime
prefixes have no adjacent gaps both at least $\epsilon D_N$.

This isolates the required local exclusion. A bound on the frequency of large
gaps alone cannot give it: even one exceptional adjacent pair affects the maximum.
Nor does divergence of $D_N$ suffice for arbitrary sequences. For the positive
sequence $g_{2j-1}=g_{2j}=j$, the corresponding ratio is exactly one at every even
prefix. This example is not a model of prime gaps or a counterexample to the
problem; it identifies precisely what an argument using only maxima would miss.

An exact finite check can compute $D_N$, the largest adjacent product, and the
largest adjacent minimum simultaneously with one pass through a prime list.
The accompanying local check verifies (1) on sieved prime prefixes; no finite
range establishes the limit.

\subsection*{FINITE COMPUTATIONAL CHECK}
For successive prime-value cutoffs $10^3,10^4,10^5,10^6$, the numbers of complete gaps are $167,1228,9591,78497$; the maximum gaps are $20,36,72,114$. The adjacent-product ratios are respectively $9/25,32/81,203/432,50/171$. Their variation is not evidence of monotonicity or a proof of a limit.

\subsection*{VERIFICATION AND REMAINING GAP}
Prove the adjacent record-scale exclusion for prime gaps. Known or computed
large individual gaps do not supply this exclusion. The next useful input must
control two neighboring gaps relative to the largest gap in the entire prefix.
