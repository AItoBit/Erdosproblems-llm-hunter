\subsection*{FORMAL RESTATEMENT}
Consider the graph on pairs $(x,y)\in\mathbb N^2$ with $\gcd(x,y)=1$, joining two pairs when exactly one coordinate changes by $1$ or $-1$. Is there a path escaping every bounded region whose vertices all satisfy $x,y>1$ and have at least one composite coordinate? Further variants ask for such a monotone path, and for a monotone path whose straight runs have uniformly bounded lengths.
