\subsection*{FORMAL RESTATEMENT}
Put $P(m)=\max\{p:p\text{ prime},\ p\mid m\}$, and set
\[f(n,k)=\#\{i\in\{1,\ldots,k\}:P(n+i)>k\}.\]
For every fixed $\alpha>1$ and every integer sequence $n=n(k)$ satisfying $n=k^{\alpha+o(1)}$, must
\[f(n,k)=(1-\rho(\alpha)+o(1))k\quad(k\to\infty)\]
hold? Here $\rho$ is the Dickman function.
