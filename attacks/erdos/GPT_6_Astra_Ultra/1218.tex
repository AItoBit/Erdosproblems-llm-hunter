\subsection*{FORMAL RESTATEMENT}
Assume the Generalised Continuum Hypothesis. Put $\beta=\omega_{\omega+1}$, with $\omega_\alpha$ denoting the initial ordinal of cardinality $\aleph_\alpha$. Does the negative partition relation
\[\aleph_{\beta+1}\not\longrightarrow(\aleph_\beta,4,4,\ldots)^3\]
hold, where the list has countably infinitely many entries equal to $4$? Explicitly, the question asks for a colouring of the unordered triples from a set of size $\aleph_{\beta+1}$ by colours $0,1,2,\ldots$, with no colour-$0$ homogeneous subset of size $\aleph_\beta$ and no colour-$j$ homogeneous four-element subset for any $j\geq1$.
