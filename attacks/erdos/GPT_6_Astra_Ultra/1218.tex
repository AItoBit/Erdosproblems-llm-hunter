\section*{Erd\H{o}s Problem 1218 --- GPT 6 Astra Ultra partial attempt}


\subsection*{FORMAL RESTATEMENT}
Assume the Generalised Continuum Hypothesis. Put $\beta=\omega_{\omega+1}$, with $\omega_\alpha$ denoting the initial ordinal of cardinality $\aleph_\alpha$. Does the negative partition relation
\[\aleph_{\beta+1}\not\longrightarrow(\aleph_\beta,4,4,\ldots)^3\]
hold, where the list has countably infinitely many entries equal to $4$? Explicitly, the question asks for a colouring of the unordered triples from a set of size $\aleph_{\beta+1}$ by colours $0,1,2,\ldots$, with no colour-$0$ homogeneous subset of size $\aleph_\beta$ and no colour-$j$ homogeneous four-element subset for any $j\geq1$.

\subsection*{CONTEXT AND SCOPE}
This replaces the earlier statement-only record with an elementary mathematical attempt. The arguments below establish only their stated partial results; no novelty or full solution is claimed.

\subsection*{ATTACK PLAN AND WORK}
Put $\kappa=\aleph_\beta$ and $\theta=\kappa^+$. The desired coloring is
equivalent to a countable family of $K_4^{(3)}$-free 3-uniform hypergraphs on
$\theta$ vertices whose union has no independent set of size $\kappa$.
The positive color classes give such a family. Conversely color each covered
triple by its first covering hypergraph, and all other triples zero; each
positive class remains $K_4^{(3)}$-free as a subhypergraph.

Under the stated GCH, this construction cannot use only countably colorable
positive hypergraphs. If each has a proper weak coloring $c_j:V\to\mathbb N$
(no monochromatic edge), the vector $(c_j(v))_{j\ge1}$ colors the union with
at most $2^{\aleph_0}=\aleph_1$ colors. Regularity of $\theta>\aleph_1$ implies
one vector class has size $\theta$ and contains no edge of the union, contradicting
the required absence of even a $\kappa$-sized independent set.

The distinction between being $K_4^{(3)}$-free and being countably colorable is
therefore essential. Substituting the latter property merely because it is
easy to arrange would impose an obstruction incompatible with the target.
The proof uses GCH only for the small size of the vector-color range.

\subsection*{VERIFICATION AND REMAINING GAP}
Construct the countable covering family with sufficiently high chromatic
complexity, or otherwise establish the negative partition relation. The
reformulation and obstruction do not supply that family.
