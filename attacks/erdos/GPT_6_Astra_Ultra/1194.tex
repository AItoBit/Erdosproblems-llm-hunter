\subsection*{FORMAL RESTATEMENT}
Suppose $A\subseteq\mathbb N$ has exactly one ordered pair $(a_n,b_n)\in A^2$ satisfying $a_n-b_n=n$ for every positive integer $n$. Determine the growth that the ratios $a_n/n$ must exhibit, and how slowly they can grow across such perfect difference sets.

\subsection*{SOURCE CONVENTIONS AND OPEN DETAILS}
Here a_n is the larger member of the pair representing the difference n; it is not the nth element of A. The source is an open-ended growth question, not an assertion that the ratios increase monotonically.
