\section*{Erd\H{o}s Problem 1194 --- GPT 6 Astra Ultra partial attempt}


\subsection*{FORMAL RESTATEMENT}
Suppose $A\subseteq\mathbb N$ has exactly one ordered pair $(a_n,b_n)\in A^2$ satisfying $a_n-b_n=n$ for every positive integer $n$. Determine the growth that the ratios $a_n/n$ must exhibit, and how slowly they can grow across such perfect difference sets.

\subsection*{CONTEXT AND SCOPE}
This replaces the earlier statement-only record with an elementary mathematical attempt. The arguments below establish only their stated partial results; no novelty or full solution is claimed.

Here a_n is the larger member of the pair representing the difference n; it is not the nth element of A. The source is an open-ended growth question, not an assertion that the ratios increase monotonically.

\subsection*{ATTACK PLAN AND WORK}
There is a precise transfer from sparse truncations to large representing
endpoints. Let $t=A(X)$. At most $\binom t2$ positive differences can be
represented with larger endpoint at most $X$, because every such pair lies in
$A\cap[1,X]$ and all positive differences are unique. Put
$M=\binom t2+1$. Among the differences $1,\ldots,M$, at least one therefore has
\[a_n>X,\qquad n\le M,\qquad \frac{a_n}{n}>\frac X{\binom{A(X)}2+1}.\tag{1}\]
This is an exact inequality, not an assertion that the function $a_n/n$ is monotone.

For example, if along some $X_j\to\infty$ one has
$A(X_j)\le K\sqrt{X_j/\log X_j}$, then (1) forces
$a_{n_j}/n_j\gg_K\log X_j\ge\log n_j$ up to a fixed constant for large $j$.
The indices $n_j$ must be unbounded, since each of finitely many endpoints
$a_n$ is fixed while $X_j$ diverges. This proves the deduction from a logarithmic
sparsity input; it does not independently prove that input.

The official page describes such sparsity results and substantially stronger
subsequent endpoint bounds. They are context, not achievements of this short
argument. Equation (1) identifies exactly which counting estimate would have
to be strengthened to improve this particular route.
