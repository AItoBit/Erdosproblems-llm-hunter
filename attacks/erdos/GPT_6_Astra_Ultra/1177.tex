\section*{Erd\H{o}s Problem 1177 --- GPT 6 Astra Ultra partial attempt}


\subsection*{FORMAL RESTATEMENT}
For a finite $3$-uniform hypergraph $G$, write $F_G(\kappa)$ for the class of $G$-free $3$-uniform hypergraphs whose chromatic number is exactly $\kappa$. Determine whether all three assertions hold:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $F_G(\aleph_1)\ne\varnothing$ implies the existence of a member with at most $2^{2^{\aleph_0}}$ vertices.
\item For finite $G,H$, nonemptiness of both $F_G(\aleph_1)$ and $F_H(\aleph_1)$ implies $F_G(\aleph_1)\cap F_H(\aleph_1)\ne\varnothing$.
\item For uncountable cardinals $\kappa,\lambda$, nonemptiness of $F_G(\kappa)$ implies nonemptiness of $F_G(\lambda)$.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{CONTEXT AND SCOPE}
This replaces the earlier statement-only record with an elementary mathematical attempt. The arguments below establish only their stated partial results; no novelty or full solution is claimed.

These concern exact chromatic cardinals, not merely lower bounds. Later external candidate-solution claims were not verified in this attempt and are not adopted as a status change.

\subsection*{ATTACK PLAN AND WORK}
There is a natural simultaneous-avoidance construction whose exact limitation
can be proved. For 3-uniform hypergraphs $X,Y$, take vertices $V(X)\times V(Y)$;
three vertices form an edge when both coordinate projections are edges.
Projection is then a hypergraph homomorphism to each factor.

Call a finite 3-uniform hypergraph pair-covered if every pair of its vertices
is in some edge. If such a hypergraph $G$ embeds in the product, its projection
to either factor is injective: two distinct vertices belong to an edge, whose
three coordinate entries must be distinct. The projection therefore embeds
$G$ in each factor. Consequently, if $G,H$ are pair-covered, $X$ is $G$-free,
and $Y$ is $H$-free, then this product is simultaneously $G$-free and $H$-free.

The chromatic direction is the wrong one for assertion two. Pulling back a
proper coloring from either factor gives
\[\chi(X\times Y)\le\min(\chi(X),\chi(Y)),\]
but supplies no lower bound. In particular, the construction does not preserve
chromatic number $\aleph_1$ merely from having that number in both factors.
For forbidden graphs that are not pair-covered, even the injectivity step can
fail. Disjoint union is not a remedy: it retains forbidden configurations
already occurring in either component.

\subsection*{VERIFICATION AND REMAINING GAP}
Prove a simultaneous avoidance construction with the exact required chromatic
number for arbitrary finite forbidden hypergraphs. The size bound in assertion
one and transfer across all uncountable cardinals in assertion three also remain open here.
