\subsection*{FORMAL RESTATEMENT}
For integers $r,e\geq3$, let $\mathcal F_{r,d,e}$ comprise the $r$-uniform hypergraphs having $d$ vertices and $e$ edges. Write $\operatorname{ex}_r(n,\mathcal F)$ for the largest number of edges in an $n$-vertex $r$-uniform hypergraph containing no member of $\mathcal F$ as a subhypergraph. Define
\[d_r(e)=\min\{d:\operatorname{ex}_r(n,\mathcal F_{r,d,e})=o(n^2)\text{ as }n\to\infty\}.\]
The Brown--Erd\H{o}s--S\'os conjecture asks whether $d_r(e)=(r-2)e+3$ for every $r,e\geq3$.
