\subsection*{FORMAL RESTATEMENT}
For every red--blue colouring of $\mathcal P([n])$, consider the largest monochromatic subfamily closed under both binary union and binary intersection. Let $f(n)$ be the minimum of this largest size over all such colourings. Define $F(n)$ in the same way with only closure under union required. Estimate both functions. In particular, are the inequalities
\[F(n)\geq n^{\omega(n)}\quad\text{for some }\omega(n)\to\infty,
\qquad F(n)=\exp(o(n))\]
valid?
