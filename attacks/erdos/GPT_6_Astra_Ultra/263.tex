\section{WORK: AN EXCEPTIONAL CRITICAL BASE}
Let $s_1=2$ and $s_{n+1}=s_n^2-s_n+1$. Induction using $s_{n+1}-1=s_n(s_n-1)$ gives
\[
\sum_{i=1}^n\frac1{s_i}=1-\frac1{s_{n+1}-1},
\qquad \sum_{i\ge1}\frac1{s_i}=1.
\]
Write $\delta_n=\log(1-1/s_n+1/s_n^2)=O(1/s_n)$. The recurrence gives
\[
2^{-n}\log s_n\longrightarrow\log\alpha
=\tfrac12\log2+\sum_{j\ge1}2^{-j-1}\delta_j.
\]
This base exceeds one: since $s_{n+1}\ge s_n^2/2$ and $s_2=3$, induction gives $s_n\ge2(3/2)^{2^{n-2}}$ for $n\ge2$. Finally,
\[
\log s_n-2^n\log\alpha
=-\sum_{j\ge n}2^{n-j-1}\delta_j=O(1/s_n)\to0.
\]
Hence $s_n\sim\alpha^{2^n}$. Taking $b_n=s_n$ shows that $\lfloor\alpha^{2^n}\rfloor$ is \emph{not} Type-2, despite lying exactly on the quadratic-growth boundary. This is the standard Sylvester-sequence obstruction, here verified directly.
