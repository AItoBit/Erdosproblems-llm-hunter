\subsection*{FORMAL RESTATEMENT}
Is there a constant $c>0$ such that for every sufficiently large $N$, the first $N$ positive cubes contain a Sidon subset of at least $cN$ elements? Is there an infinite set $A\subseteq\mathbb N$ of positive density for which $\{a^3:a\in A\}$ is Sidon? Here Sidon means uniqueness of every two-term sum up to exchanging its summands.
