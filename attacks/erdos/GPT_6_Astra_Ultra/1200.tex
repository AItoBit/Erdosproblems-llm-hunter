\section*{Erd\H{o}s Problem 1200 --- GPT 6 Astra Ultra partial attempt}


\subsection*{FORMAL RESTATEMENT}
Does an absolute constant $C>0$ exist such that, for all sufficiently large $x$, one can select distinct primes $p_1<\cdots<p_k<x$ and residues $a_i\pmod{p_i}$ satisfying
\[\sum_{i=1}^k\frac1{p_i}<C,\qquad
[1,x)\cap\mathbb Z\subseteq\bigcup_{i=1}^k(a_i+p_i\mathbb Z)?\]

\subsection*{CONTEXT AND SCOPE}
This replaces the earlier statement-only record with an elementary mathematical attempt. The arguments below establish only their stated partial results; no novelty or full solution is claimed.

The page says every integer n<x; the finite positive interval is the intended interpretation of this covering question.

\subsection*{ATTACK PLAN AND WORK}
Let $M=\lceil x\rceil-1$, so the interval to be covered is $\{1,\ldots,M\}$.
One class modulo $p_i$ covers at most $M/p_i+1$ of these integers. Therefore
every proposed cover satisfies
\[1\le\sum_{i=1}^k\frac1{p_i}+\frac kM.\tag{1}\]
In particular a construction using $o(M)$ primes needs reciprocal mass at least
$1-o(1)$. The usual fact $\pi(x)=o(x)$ gives this condition for the available
primes as well; it is only a necessary lower threshold, not an impossibility result.

There is also an exact greedy estimate for any prescribed distinct primes.
If $U$ integers remain uncovered, averaging over the $p$ residue classes finds
one covering at least $U/p$ of them. Choosing such a class successively gives
\[U_{\rm final}\le M\prod_i(1-1/p_i).\tag{2}\]
No independence of the uncovered residue patterns is assumed in this averaging.
However (2) need not be below one when the reciprocal sum is bounded: for
large selected primes the product is of constant scale, leaving an upper bound
of order $M$. Failure of this bound to prove coverage is not a lower bound on
what a better choice of residues can cover.

\subsection*{VERIFICATION AND REMAINING GAP}
Exploit correlated choices of residues to cover the whole interval with
bounded reciprocal mass. The necessary mass bound and elementary greedy
upper bound on leftovers do not construct such a cover or disprove its existence.
