\subsection*{FORMAL RESTATEMENT}
For fixed integers $t,r\geq2$, let $K_t(r)$ have $t$ vertex classes of size $r$, with every transversal $t$-set an edge. Is
\[
\operatorname{ex}_t(n,K_t(r))\geq n^{\,t-r^{1-t}-o(1)}
\qquad(n\to\infty)
\]
always true?
